Visualization summary · four accepted population trajectories and one separate trial

The full campaign is still generating. This page summarizes existing completed examples; it is not a live progress counter or a final dataset report. Independent numerical qualification was waived; the selected population files passed runtime checks and full readback.

MetricsOverviewSeparate trialHistorical visualizationsReport guide

29,844 physical states · 4,096 saved frames · 0.5 ms sampling · 2.0475 s per full trajectory.

Current animations show the first 0.20 s with 10x displacement and 6.00 s playback. Geometry remains at its actual scale, all axis units are equal, light-gray lines mark the physical exterior, and colors show actual normal displacement in mm. Histories and metrics use all 4,096 frames.

The same-initial-condition linear tangent reference is analytic. Relative response differences include phase accumulation and integration error; they are not a percentage of nonlinearity. The four examples are illustrative and do not characterize the whole population.
